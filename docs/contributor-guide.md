# Contributor guide

For creator marketing managers, TikTok Shop managers, social commerce managers and anyone else who has made a gift box for creators.

## What to send

1. **A video link** (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube Shorts) showing the box being opened. A creator's unboxing or your own brand video both work.
2. **Brand and category.**
3. **Month you sent it.**
4. **What is inside**, if the video does not show everything.
5. **Permission:** a clear yes to feature the box in the library with a link back to the original post, and how you would like to be credited (or not).

If you have the original video file or clear photos of the box, send those too: they give better angles than a short slice of a video.

## What qualifies

A designed box, mailer, bag or insert that a designer could learn from. Plain shipping cartons and retail boxes do not get a card. The full rules are in `quality-bar.md`.

## What you get

- A card with story-style screenshots, an item breakdown with reference images, why the concept fits your brand, and take-aways.
- Credit if you want it.

## What we will not do

- Show view counts, likes or follower numbers.
- Rank your box against others.
- Keep your box listed if you ask us to remove it.

## Intake

For now, links are collected by direct message or comment and logged in `data/submissions/submissions.csv`. A form (for example a Google Form with the fields above) can replace this later.

## Message template

> Hi, this is the video for our [brand] gift box: [link]. Category: [category]. Sent in [month]. Inside: [items]. You have my permission to feature it with a link back to the original post. Credit: [name / company / no credit].
