# Product vision

## The problem

Creator gifting is one of the few moments a brand controls the first on-camera impression of its product, and the box is the stage. Yet when a team designs a gift box, there is no shared reference: designers see retail packaging, marketers see engagement dashboards, and the boxes that creators actually film are scattered across thousands of short videos. Teams reinvent the same ideas, or ship a plain carton and hope.

## The product

A visual library of real creator gift boxes, organized so a marketer or packaging designer can answer three questions in minutes:

1. **What do good boxes look like?** Screenshots from every angle, closed to fully unpacked, in a story view and a flat lay.
2. **What goes in, and why?** An item-by-item breakdown with a reference image for each item, plus why the concept fits that brand and product.
3. **What should I borrow?** Short take-aways and a plain-language note on how creators reacted.

## Who it is for

- **Creator marketing managers** planning seasonal and launch gifting.
- **TikTok Shop and social commerce managers** who ship boxes to affiliates and need creators to film them.
- **Packaging and brand designers** looking for unboxing-first ideas.
- **Agencies and influencer platforms** who brief brands on gifting.

## What makes it different

- It is built from what creators actually filmed, not from brand press images.
- It is honest about evidence: reception is a directional rating with an evidence label, never a vanity metric.
- It is contributed to by the people who do this work, which keeps it current and diverse across categories.

## What it is not

- Not a performance leaderboard. Views, likes and followers are deliberately not shown; the sample is not exhaustive and a single post can mislead.
- Not a price or supplier directory.
- Not a place for plain shipping cartons or retail boxes.

## Success looks like

Near term (private prototype to 100 boxes):
- 100 designed boxes across at least 7 categories, each with at least 5 distinct angles and an item breakdown.
- A repeatable capture process that adds a box in minutes, not hours.
- Contributors from at least 15 brands.

Later (public):
- Marketers say they used it to brief a box design or defend a gifting budget.
- Contributors keep sending boxes without being asked.

## Principles

1. Inspiration, not ranking.
2. Every claim is grounded in a frame you can see.
3. Qualitative over numerical when data is incomplete.
4. Credit the source, and remove on request.
5. Simple to navigate: search, a few filters, one box at a time.

## Risks to manage

- **Rights and consent:** screenshots come from third-party videos. See `rights-and-ethics.md`.
- **Selection bias:** TikTok-heavy, English-language, and skewed toward boxes that are visible and filmable.
- **Scale of manual work:** capture is the bottleneck; see `roadmap.md` for the plan to automate it.
