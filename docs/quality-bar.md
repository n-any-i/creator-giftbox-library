# Quality bar: what gets a card

The project owner removed a plain retail shoe box and asked why it was there. This page exists so that does not happen again.

## Include

- A **designed** box, mailer, bag, sleeve or insert: custom shape, staged reveal (ribbon, slide, layers), printed lid or card, keepsake or bonus item, personal touch, themed concept, partner collaboration.
- A video that shows **the exterior, the opening, and what is inside**, even if only partly.
- A box a designer could learn something from.

## Skip

- A plain brown shipping carton holding retail boxes (for example a bulk PR drop).
- A doorstep stack of retail cases.
- A sealed carton whose contents never appear.
- A retail box with no extra packaging (for example a standard shoe box).
- A video where nothing of the box is visible in the sampled seconds, after one later-window retry.

## Borderline: decide with the owner

- A branded shipping carton with a printed pattern (kept once, for lululemon, as an exterior-only entry).
- Plain retail packaging kept deliberately as a labelled contrast (AG1, The Ordinary). Tag it "Plain retail packaging" and say why. Do not add more without asking.

## Every entry must have

- 4 to 9 frames with story-style captions.
- An item list with a reference frame per item (or "not shown").
- A concept that ties the box to the brand and product.
- A source link to the original post.
- A reception rating or "Not enough data", with an evidence label.

## Image definition

Screenshots must be readable: a designer should be able to read printed text and see textures. Do not downsize or re-compress frames. If a frame is too soft to use, replace it with a sharper one rather than keeping it.

## Every entry must not have

- View, like or follower counts anywhere in the page text.
- Claims about items that never appear on camera.
- Long copied text from packaging; keep to short phrases.
